Two separate fixes
Parser: the shared parser reads dates month-first for day-first tenants. App and email reports, and forms without the booking-v3 hint mismatch, fall into this group. Parse with the tenant’s declared format. Verify ambiguous dates and the relevant entry routes.
Hint: booking-v3 tells a day-first tenant to type MM/DD/YYYY, while the form reads the date in the tenant’s order. Derive the hint and input interpretation from one format rule, then verify that following the hint preserves the intended day and month.
